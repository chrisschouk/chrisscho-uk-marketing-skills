# Independent Artist Growth & Release Execution Skill

A strategic marketing and release framework for independent artists, bands, and DIY record labels to maximize release impact, algorithmic push, and radio airplay.

---

## 1. The 6-Week Release Timeline

### Week -6 to -4: Pre-Release Setup
- Submit track to **Spotify for Artists** editorial pitch at least 14 days before release.
- Upload un-mastered/mastered audio to press portals (Total Audio Promo, Submithub, Groover).
- Pitch **BBC Introducing** / local independent radio stations.

### Week -2 to -1: Fan Teasers & Presales
- Launch shortform visual teasers (behind-the-scenes, studio process, acoustic hook).
- Drive pre-saves to boost Release Radar algorithmic triggering on Day 1.

### Release Week (Day 0 - Day 7): Active Execution
- Post release announcement thread + direct streaming links.
- Send release press kit to radio producers and music blogs.
- Engage with every comment and share user-generated content.

### Week +2 to +4: Momentum & Algorithmic Scaling
- Monitor Spotify for Artists **Save Rate** (Target > 30%) and **Repeat Listener Ratio**.
- Pitch mid-tier independent curators for genre-specific playlists.

---

## 2. Spotify Algorithmic Triggers

- **Save Rate (Saves / Streams)**: Higher priority than stream count. A 35% save rate signals high intent to Discover Weekly algorithms.
- **Completion Rate**: High listener retention during the first 30 seconds prevents algorithmic drop-off.
- **User Playlist Additions**: Adds to personal user playlists carry 5x the algorithmic weight of temporary editorial placements.

---

## 3. Radio Pitching Blueprint

### Pitch Email Structure for Radio Producers:
1. **Subject Line**: `[New Music] Artist Name - Track Title (Genre/Location)`
2. **Paragraph 1**: 2-sentence hook (Sound-alike references, key career milestone, radio support to date).
3. **Paragraph 2**: Private streaming link (SoundCloud private link or MP3 download, no ZIP files or heavy attachments).
4. **Paragraph 3**: Brief artist bio + social links.
