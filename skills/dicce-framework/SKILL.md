# DICCE Framework for Product Marketing & Launches

The **DICCE Framework** is a high-converting product marketing architecture designed to turn complex technical products into compelling, high-converting launch campaigns.

---

## The 5 Pillars of DICCE

### 1. D – Desire (The Transformation)
- What is the immediate, visceral outcome the target user wants?
- **Avoid**: Vague promises ("Improve productivity").
- **Use**: Specific outcomes ("Deploy 4 isolated cloud desktops in under 1 second").

### 2. I – Interest (The Unique Mechanism)
- What is the surprising technical or functional mechanism that makes this possible?
- Why does the old way fail, and why does this new mechanism succeed?
- Example: "Instead of brittle DOM scrapers, OpenDesktop uses vision-driven virtual framebuffers over WebSockets."

### 3. C – Connection (Authentic Narrative)
- Who built this and why?
- Build-in-public transparency, real practitioner voice, zero corporate fluff.
- Share real engineering trade-offs, benchmarks, and honest design decisions.

### 4. C – Clarity (Zero Ambiguity)
- If a 10-year-old reads the headline, do they know what the product does?
- Explicit technical specifications: language, framework, API protocols, pricing, dependencies.

### 5. E – Execution (Frictionless Call to Action)
- What is the ONE single next step for the user?
- `pip install open-desktop`, `git clone`, or `View Live Demo on chrisscho.uk`.

---

## DICCE Audit Checklist

When reviewing any landing page, README, or marketing post:
- [ ] **Desire**: Does the hero section promise a concrete, desired outcome?
- [ ] **Interest**: Is the unique mechanism clearly highlighted within 10 seconds?
- [ ] **Connection**: Does the copy sound like a real engineer speaking to another practitioner?
- [ ] **Clarity**: Are vagueness and buzzwords completely removed?
- [ ] **Execution**: Is there a single, clear call to action?
